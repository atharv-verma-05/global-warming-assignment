Method in short

Each series is forecast with three models: a linear trend fitted to the last 10 years, a quadratic trend fitted to the last 20 years, and damped Holt exponential smoothing. To check them, the dashboard hides the most recent years, forecasts one and two years ahead, and compares against what actually happened (a rolling-origin backtest). The ensemble weights each model by the inverse of its backtest error, and the shaded band is an approximate 95% range taken from the ensemble's backtest error. A "persistence" forecast (next year equals this year) is the baseline each model must beat. Sea level is different: it is a physics-style curve anchored to NASA's latest reading, rate and acceleration, so it has no backtest.
